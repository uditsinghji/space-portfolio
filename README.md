### 🧪 Master's Project: Test Bench Design for TVC Rocket, Universität Bremen  
- Designed and validated a modular, test bench for characterizing TVC Rocket in order to analyse Gimbal Mechanism.
- **Engineering:** Performed structural analysis to ensure bench integrity under thermal cycling. 

### 🚀 Thruster Plume Backflow (CFD + DSMC Coupling)
- Working on a continuum-to-rarefied coupling pipeline (OpenFOAM → DSMC).
- Captured particle backflow around a 0.5 mm nozzle lip—a known contamination risk in space.
- Validated with MATLAB; runs efficiently on a standard laptop.

### 📡 Space Safety Mission Control Dashboard
- Deployed a live, interactive Streamlit tool for collision probability, conjunction analysis (ESA PC thresholds), relative motion (Clohessy-Wiltshire), and tumbling debris capture.
- Uses Monte Carlo simulations and Euler rigid-body dynamics.
- **Impact:** Reduces risk assessment time from hours to seconds. 

### 🛰️ CubeSat Payload Bracket (ToulouseEye)
- Designed a flight-ready bracket (AL6061-T6) for a 3U Earth observation CubeSat (10 m GSD).
- Withstands 12g launch loads, maintains optical alignment <0.1°, and keeps camera <60°C.
- Safety factor >15, first modal frequency 5299 Hz (SolidWorks + Ansys Workbench).

### 🌙 Lunar Night Thermal Survival
- Simulated a 336-hour lunar night (-173°C) using conjugate heat transfer (SimFlow, OpenFOAM).
- Proved passive survival (255 K core) using a passive MLI blanket (ε=0.05), eliminating the need for active heating hardware and saving mass/power.
